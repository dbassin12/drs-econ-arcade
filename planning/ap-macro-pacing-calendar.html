Sick-day protocol: 7 days, spent like an economist

Every meeting in the day-by-day map has a colored dot for what happens if you’re out that day. Seven sub kits, built from files already in your Econ folder, let a green or yellow day run without you. The full playbook is in your folder.

33 GREEN: ideal sub days; the kit runs itself 64 YELLOW: sub-able; the lesson moves to the next green day 17 RED: protect Day 1, tests, mocks, Mar 31, May 3–6 16 FREE: after May 7, seniors gone

The 4 re-sync rules

  • Green day missed: nothing to do. The kit replaced the drill.
  • Yellow day missed: the next green day in that unit becomes the missed lesson, and the drill it replaces shrinks into Do Nows.
  • Two or more missed in one unit: fold the review day into the test warm-up and let the test slide one day at most. Tests anchor to dates, so nothing later moves.
  • Red day threatened: swap with the next day. A test slides one day at most and a mock shifts within its week. If May 3 to 6 is hit, a sub proctors timed sets.

Green days that can absorb a sick day: U1: 5 · U2: 1 · U3: 5 · U4: 6 · U5: 7 · U6: 4 · Micro: 1 · Review: 4 · Final sprint: 0.
